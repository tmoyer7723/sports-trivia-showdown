        📋 How to play

        1. One player creates a room and shares the code or invite link.
        2. At least 2 people join from their own devices. The host presses Start.
        3. Everyone gets the same 10 sports questions, each with four choices and 15 seconds to answer.
        4. Each player may answer only once per question. Correct answers score 1,000 points in 0–3 seconds, 800 in 4–6 seconds, 600 in 7–10 seconds, or 400 in 11–15 seconds. Wrong or missed answers score zero.
        5. When everyone answers or time runs out, the correct answer and leaderboard appear. The host advances the game.
        6. After 10 questions, the highest total score wins. Tied scores are shared places.

        Timing and scores are checked by the game server. If you leave or disconnect, you lose your spot in the current game.
